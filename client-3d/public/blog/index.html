Connection fixes ┬─┬ノ( º _ ºノ)

17 August 2026

  • [fixed] Logging in and joining rooms failed for most people — traffic between our CDN and the game server was quietly being dropped on the floor. Everything now runs over a direct tunnel instead.
  • [fixed] The game server could be knocked offline by a single malformed request from the open internet. Those get turned away at the door now.
  • [changed] Pages and music load quicker across the board — connections are made at the nearest edge instead of reaching all the way to the server every time.
  • [new] This page. The version number tucked in the corner of the club links here now, instead of nowhere at all.

Playlists & smoother music ┏(・o･)┛♪

18 July 2026

  • [new] Import a whole YouTube playlist by pasting its URL — in My Playlists, or MutantTube on the desktop.
  • [changed] Audio reactivity is worked out up front on the server, so the visuals stay locked to the beat without every machine in the room crunching audio.
  • [changed] Scrubbing and skipping around a track is far quicker — cached audio serves partial requests properly now.

The resident DJ gets some manners ヾ(⌐■_■)ノ♪

11 July 2026

  • [new] The NPC DJ hands the booth over when a real person wants to play, and quietly picks it back up when the queue runs dry.
  • [new] Room owners can flip the NPC DJ on and off without restarting the room.
  • [changed] New default set — Mutant Rotation retires House Rotation.

NPC DJ presence ┌(・。・)┘♪

10 July 2026

  • [new] The NPC DJ actually walks to the booth to play, and wanders the dancefloor between sets, rather than blinking into existence behind the decks.
  • [new] Pick the NPC DJ mode when you create a room: off, fill-in, or resident.
  • [new] They say something in chat when taking over or handing the decks back.
  • [fixed] Other players no longer flicker into the wrong spot for a moment when you arrive.

Music sync ♪(´▽｀)

6 July 2026

  • [fixed] Turning up mid-track now drops you at the right point in the song instead of slowly drifting out of sync with everyone else.
  • [fixed] A pile of DJ queue gremlins — the rotation resetting itself, players stuck holding an empty turn, and tracks lying about how long they were.
